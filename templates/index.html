ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making Plant Growth Measurable

Smart Microgreen AI uses computer vision to extract vegetation-related features from fenugreek microgreen images. These features are combined into a Visual Growth Index that allows experimental boxes to be monitored and compared across observation days.
